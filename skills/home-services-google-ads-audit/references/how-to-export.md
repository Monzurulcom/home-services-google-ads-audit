# How to export the two reports from Google Ads

Both take about two minutes. Google moves menus around now and then, so if a button has a
slightly different name, look for the nearest match.

## 1. Campaigns report

1. Sign in at ads.google.com and pick the account.
2. In the left menu, open **Campaigns**.
3. Set the date range (top right) to **Last 30 days**.
4. Click the **Columns** icon above the table, then **Modify columns**, and make sure these are
   ticked: Campaign type, Bid strategy type, Clicks, Cost, Conversions, Search lost IS (budget),
   Search lost IS (rank). The last two are under "Competitive metrics". Click **Apply**.
5. Click the **Download** icon above the table and choose **.csv**.

## 2. Search terms report

1. In the left menu, open **Insights and reports**, then **Search terms**. (In some accounts
   it is under Campaigns > Insights and reports.)
2. Keep the date range at **Last 30 days**.
3. Under **Columns**, make sure Match type, Added/Excluded, Clicks, Cost and Conversions are showing.
4. Click **Download** and choose **.csv**.

## Then

Attach both files to the chat. Nothing else is needed: no password and no account access.
